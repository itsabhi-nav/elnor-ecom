The SPAO ritual

Fifteen minutes, three stages.

A spa session follows an order: warm the body, release the tension, then rest. Every SPAO device is built around the same rhythm.

01 — WARM

Warm

Gentle heat relaxes muscles and prepares the body, the way a hot bath opens a spa visit.

≈ 5 min
02 — RELEASE

Release

Kneading, pressure or percussion works into stiff areas at the intensity you choose.

≈ 7 min
03 — RESTORE

Restore

Slow, light pulses as the session winds down. Breathe, sit still, and let the calm settle.

≈ 3 min

Origins

Borrowed from three spa traditions.

SPAO takes its cues from rituals people have trusted for generations, then makes them small enough to fit on your desk or bedside.

温泉

Onsen · Japan

Natural hot springs where warmth comes first. It is why every SPAO session begins with heat.

찜질방

Jjimjilbang · Korea

Korea's social bathhouses, built for slow, unhurried recovery. SPAO is designed to be used daily, not saved for weekends.

指圧

Shiatsu · Japan

Finger-pressure therapy that targets precise points. It shapes how SPAO places its nodes and pressure zones.

How often can I use a SPAO device?

Most people enjoy one or two 15-minute sessions a day. Start on a low setting and increase gradually. If you are pregnant, have a medical condition or a pacemaker, check with your doctor first.
